RDC yihanangirije abayobozi b'ihuriro C64 ko bashobora gufungwa bazira guhura na Kabila

2026-11-05

Minisitiri w'ubutegetsi bw'igihugu muri Repubulika ya Demokarasi ya Congo, Jacquemain Shabani, yihanangirije abanyamuryango b'ihuriro ry'abatavuga rumwe n'ubutegetsi rya C64, ababurira ko bashobora guhanishwa igifungo kiri hagati y'umwaka umwe n'imyaka itanu baramutse bagiye mu mujyi wa Goma, mu burasirazuba bw'igihugu, guhura na Joseph Kabila wahoze ari Perezida w'icyo gihugu.
