Nigeria: Umwami gakondo yahagaritswe ku mirimo ye nyuma yo kuregwa ihohotera rishingiye ku gitsina

2026-11-25

Umwe mu bami gakondo bazwi cyane muri Nigeria, Emir wa Daura, yahagaritswe mu kazi no mu ngendo z'akazi, nyuma y'uko umugenzi w'umukerarugendo ukomoka muri Pologne amushinje kumukorera ihohotera rishingiye ku gitsina.
