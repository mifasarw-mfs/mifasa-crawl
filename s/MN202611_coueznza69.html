U$4.97M Secured For Climate Adaptation in Gatsibo, Nyagatare, Kayonza

2026-11-03

The government of Rwanda has secured a U$4.97Million from the Adaptation Fund to strengthen climate resilience among smallholder farmers in Kayonza,Gatsibo and Nyagatare districts. The project aims at improving access to climate information and climate-smart farming technologies,benefiting at least 10,000 farmers.
